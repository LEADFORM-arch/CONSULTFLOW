# ConsultFlow interface system

## Intent

- Human: a US-based independent strategy consultant, moving between client calls and running the practice without an operations team.
- Task: understand the next best action, protect booked revenue, and move each client engagement forward.
- Feel: boardroom calm, confidential, decisive, and financially precise.

## Product world

Engagements, client briefs, retainers, decision logs, revenue protection, deliverables, and follow-through.

## Signature

The Engagement Rail unifies meeting stage, payment confidence, qualification, and next action. It is the product's primary operating view.

## Surface system

1. Canvas: warm stone `#f4f2ed`
2. Base paper: `#fbfaf7`
3. Raised paper: `#ffffff`
4. Overlay: raised paper with a deep ink shadow
5. Focus: cobalt ring with 3px offset

Use warm paper for the workspace and deep ink for navigation. Separate surfaces with low-opacity borders, not heavy shadows.

## Palette

- Ink navy `#101b2a`: confidentiality and authority
- Paper `#fbfaf7`: client-document warmth
- Cobalt `#24529a`: decisions and primary actions
- Ledger green `#1d725b`: secured revenue and healthy states
- Muted brass `#a47635`: premium detail and attention
- Warning umber `#ad642d`: risk without alarmism

## Typography and data

Geist Sans for product copy. Geist Mono for currency, times, scores, and compact operational data. Use 14px as the working base, 12px for metadata, and restrained display headings.

## Spacing

4px base with a working rhythm of 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, and 40px. Dense rows use 12–16px vertical padding; executive summaries use 24–32px.

## Reusable patterns

- Navigation follows the client lifecycle: Briefing, Schedule, Engagements, Clients, Revenue, Intelligence.
- Primary actions are cobalt with restrained radius (8px), never pill-shaped.
- Status labels use pale semantic backgrounds and tabular typography.
- Avoid repeated KPI cards. Use a single Firm Pulse ledger or content-specific analytical surfaces.
- Inputs are slightly inset and darker than their parent surface.
